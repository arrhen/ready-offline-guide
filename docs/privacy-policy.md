# Ready: Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: [date]

Ready collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Your files.** You can add text, Markdown and PDF files so Ready can search them. Ready keeps only the text
it reads from them, in a database on your phone. It does not copy the original file, and it never uploads,
shares or exports your files or their text. By default the database is excluded from iCloud and device
backups. You can include it in Settings. You can delete everything at any time in Settings.

**Network use.** This version of Ready makes no network requests of its own. Optional tips are handled by
Apple through the App Store; Ready does not see your payment details. If you choose to open a link, such as
GitHub Sponsors or an email to report a problem, that happens outside Ready, in your browser or mail app.

**Email.** If you choose "Report a problem", Ready opens a draft email addressed to privacy@arrhen.me. Nothing
is sent unless you send it.

**Children.** Ready is not directed at children.

**Changes.** If this changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.

**Contact.** privacy@arrhen.me. Ready is free, open-source software provided as is, under the MIT licence, at
https://github.com/arrhen/ready-offline-guide.
